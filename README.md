# Mary Kay Client Studio

A beautiful, pink macOS-style customer management app for Mary Kay Beauty Sales Consultants in Malaysia.

## Features
- **Clients** — track name, WhatsApp number, notes, and client status (New / Regular / VIP / Repeat buyer), with live purchase totals per client.
- **Sales** — record what each client purchased, quantity, price (RM), sale date, and delivery date.
- **Follow-up reminders** — saving a sale automatically schedules **1-week, 2-week and 1-month** check-ins. Review what's due today, overdue, or upcoming; mark done, snooze +7 days, copy a warm WhatsApp message, or send it straight to the client.
- **Products** — editable Mary Kay catalogue with indicative RM prices.
- **Dashboard** — revenue, sales, client and follow-up stats with trend and top-product charts.
- **Settings** — editable WhatsApp follow-up templates, browser notifications, and JSON backup/restore.

## How it works
All data is stored securely in your browser's local storage on the device you use. Use **Settings → Export backup** regularly and keep the JSON file safe — you can restore it anytime with **Import backup**.

Open the app on your phone and add it to your home screen for quick access, just like an app.
